The same label space as a flow diagram — size the bars by Size by: label mappings, distinct taxa, or the 17.4M dataset images (which tell very different stories). The living branch fans out through the Linnaean ranks (Kingdom → Species); detritus, artefact, and inert flow straight into their proposed_label values. Click a node to dive in.

Bar height & ribbon width = the selected Size by metric (label mappings · distinct taxa · dataset images) · flip Source on to insert the source-dataset column right after the root · recolour by branch or source dataset with Color by · hover a node or flow for its lineage & share · click a node to re-root.

Reading it. Every level is a column; a node's height and its outgoing ribbons are proportional to the selected Size by metric — Mappings (taxonomy rows, source-label → taxon), Taxa (distinct taxa in the subtree), or Images (real per-class sample counts from planktonzilla-17M, 17,404,047 images). The smallest siblings are pooled into a faded “+N more”; in Images mode, taxa with no dataset samples drop out. The three views diverge sharply — e.g. living dominates by taxa, but detritus is ~60% of the images.
Source. planktonzilla_dataset/planktonzilla_taxonomy.csv — mappings across datasets (NCBI, Wikidata, WHOI, EcoTaxa, ZooLake, …).
