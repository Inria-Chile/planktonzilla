## Caveats — KI-3 / KI-5 / KI-6 (what to spot-check at 18-03)

- **KI-3 (substring / fuzzy match):** the Wikidata search matches the first biological hit for the deepest known rank; a same-spelling homonym in another kingdom can be picked. Verify each `draft:wikidata` genus/species is the intended taxon.
